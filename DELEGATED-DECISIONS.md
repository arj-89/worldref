Every call the approver agent makes in Delegated mode, with its reason and its source. Append-only. The owner reads this at each phase summary and can overturn any entry; an overturned entry stays, marked overturned, and the new ruling is appended.

Sources, in the order the approver consults them: the product-design skill, this project's `DESIGN.md`, the owner's recorded decisions (chat, voice notes, prior entries here), `LESSONS.md`.

Hard stops are never decided here: anything that costs money, making a repo public, deleting data, irreversible migrations, secrets. Those pause the project and ask the owner once; the pause is logged below with `paused` as the decision.
